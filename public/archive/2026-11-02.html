周末黑客项目：HackerTube。由于对 YouTube 的推荐算法感到厌烦，我用 Claude 将 Hacker News 20 年来 4300 万条评论筛选缩减为 6,454 个被持续推荐的视频。观影体验得到了*显著*提升，使用 Opus 5.5 仅花了约 2 小时： https://hackertube.net - 无需登录即可获得极佳推荐（状态保留在本地浏览器会话中） - 封面图截取自视频中部的代表性画面，而非经过极端 A/B 测试超优化的用户上传封面 - 评论分析的底层数据打包为单个 zip 文件，你可以将其提供给自己的 Claude 来生成专属版本 - HackerTube 长视频（HackerTube Longs）✊ 关于制作流程的更多细节： 我使用的是 Claude Code（Mac 桌面端应用），默认设置为 Opus 5.5（中等思考深度）。我全程只需与 Claude 对话并让它处理所有事情，我协同完成的唯一“手动工作”都是在浏览器中进行的（类似于 MenuGen 的模式）：1) 我必须从 Google Cloud UI 创建某种 API 密钥来向 YouTube 查询视频元数据，2) 我启动了一个 VPS 服务器用于托管，3) 我购买了域名。服务器启动后，我把 IP 交给 Claude，让它更新、保护、加固、部署并优化代码，它完成了整个过程。 许多以前通常需要数周甚至数月才能完成的优秀项目，现在在一顿早餐的时间里就能“少样本（few-shot）”快速搞定。还有什么是我们尚未想到的可能性？

We’re clearly moving from a world where agents help us do our work incrementally better or faster, to us helping agents do their work. This turns everyone in one of these positions to that of a manager of a team of agents, instead of an individual contributor with just some added leverage from AI. The biggest areas of impact will be information-heavy jobs, like coding, cybersecurity, legal work, biotech, engineering, and many other fields. And as a result, in these jobs your leverage will begin to be decoupled from the normal ratio of hours spent working to output generated. For essentially the history of knowledge work the main variable you had to play with was hours of work done (assuming fixed level talent/coordination/strategy). Now we’ve separated these variables because agents can parallelize work for us, to in theory execute as much as we need to pull off whatever goal we’re trying to accomplish. The people and organizations that discover this sooner, and orient their operations around it, will see compounding gains in their workflows. This is one of the better litmus tests for whether you’re pushing your use of AI enough or not. Up until maybe six months ago probably the best you could do with AI was pull off something you already would produce, but just doing it a little faster. But now we’re entering a point where the output of working with AI can and should dramatically exceed what you would been able to do before, in a fraction of the time. The product prototype should not only be fully functional and interactive, but it should probably have meaningfully better features than it would have had before. The research for a project should consume every single fact on the subject across every dimension of analysis possible. The business process you implement with AI should operate entirely differently and learn from all its past failures in a way no human could. We’re unquestionably entering a period where AI will be smarter than us on most axes. Perhaps not on every dimension that matters (intelligence is still very jagged), but at least on enough dimensions that when we combine it with what we’re good at, you get qualitatively different outcomes. So the simple test is: if you could have done what you’re doing before AI, you’re likely not pushing it enough. It’s almost incomprehensible how wide the distance is between someone who’s working at the frontier with AI and basically everyone else right now. The crazy thing is these lines are not converging, but only growing farther apart. If the curve of AI progress had leveled off, it would certainly have given most people and companies time to catch up or implement their workflows in a standardized way. The technology would have neutralized competitive advantage because everyone would be using it the same way. But what’s happened is the opposite. The curve of AI progress is basically going vertical right now, which means if you had codified your processes 3-6 months ago you’re likely already behind again. Now imagine most companies that kicked into gear a year ago and thought that they were done. Even further behind now. I’ve never seen a technology in my life and career that improves at this rate, which consequently means you need to upgrade your expectations at a similar rate. Our brains weren’t wired for this, and our organizational change management processes certainly weren’t either. With any form of recursive self improvement, things just get wilder. It’s a time when you will need to be constantly reevaluating the things that didn’t work just a couple months ago to see if they now can be solved. AI native companies certainly have a strong advantage due to this right now, so now it comes down to can the bigger companies keep up. Wild times.

📜 原帖中文精译与推文细节
Pretty cool that we clearly have an emerging form factor for what AI might look like for the foreseeable future as autonomous systems dedicated to specific tasks or domains. This is a huge leap past the LLM wrapper days because the new expectation is that you can finally farm out more sophisticated work to the agent to tackle for you. And you can reliably trust that work can get accomplished properly (still with a human in the loop) because of far better tool and computer use, and the general intelligence of the models improving. 1) The tool and computer use capability increases in the models mean that you can provide the agent with far more granular access to external data systems and other software to operate. This means, in effect, the agent begins to execute workflows similar to how a person would have in that same function. And where there isn’t existing software, the agent just builds it itself. This is a huge unlock. 2) Next, the actual capability of the models are improving in just about every vertical domain. The models can make far better judgment calls in legal, do deeper bio research, scan for security issues in code, understand supply chain processes, and more. Obviously in many of these spaces then models still have worse intuition than people, but that will only get better. 3) Finally, the harnesses and surrounding infra — sandboxes, file systems, data pipelines, etc. — allows for longer running agents, agents to be able to work on arbitrarily complex or long tasks, process data at enormous scale, and more. Few of these components worked well for AI even a year ago, but we now have many of the core primitives necessary for more autonomous systems. When combined with the right UX, implementation, and change management for the human oversight and coordination, it’s clear that these autonomous systems will become quite powerful in a wide variety of enterprise workflows. We’re starting to see them in IT automation, cyber operations, drug discovery, manufacturing, AI research, and more spaces. You can almost imagine one or more of these systems for every role in the economy. Quite a bit of opportunity ahead.

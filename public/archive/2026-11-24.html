This is a pretty key insight. Services businesses are always held back by their ability to deliver outcomes to customers. Not to mention, the customer generally always wants the work done faster, at a lower price, or at a higher quality. You’ll see this in cyber security, advertising and marketing, legal, accounting, IT transformation, and a number of other historically people-only services industries. It’s a great time if you’re starting one of these businesses, or if you have the capability to transform an existing one. 2026 is just waking up each day to the magnitude of news that could only have happened once per decade before 2026.

📜 原帖中文精译与推文细节
This is a pretty key insight. Services businesses are always held back by their ability to deliver outcomes to customers. That scaling essentially only could come from headcount, and no matter how much demand you saw, there was always a finite amount of talent you could get for the problem you were going after. Not to mention, the customer generally always wants the work done faster, at a lower price, or at a higher quality. Agents entirely change the calculus here, because now we can scale output in a way that’s decoupled from the among of time that goes in. A single engineer can have the output of dozens of engineers before. A single designer can probably build out your entire design system and brand. And so on. Well why isn’t this just all agents then? Because the key is to still have experts on the front end that actually can ensure the right outcomes are delivered from agents. It’s likely that we’re going to see this in a wide variety of categories. You’ll see this in cyber security, advertising and marketing, legal, accounting, IT transformation, and a number of other historically people-only services industries. It’s a great time if you’re starting one of these businesses, or if you have the capability to transform an existing one. 2026 is just waking up each day to the magnitude of news that could only have happened once per decade before 2026.

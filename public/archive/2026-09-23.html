Opus 5.5 caught that the two groups' standard deviations differed more than 100-fold, re-ran it the right way, and found the dry-season difference didn't hold up after all. This accuracy gain came with a final answer that was half the length of Opus 5's, and also needed 78% fewer tokens end to end.
